USF resources the bot knows about

CC

Counseling Center

Confidential counseling for enrolled USF students, including individual and group sessions and crisis support.

Use it when: stress, anxiety, low mood, or personal concerns are getting in the way of daily life.

AS

Academic Success Center

Tutoring, writing support, and learning-strategy help for many USF courses.

Use it when: you're stuck in a class, preparing for exams, or want feedback on a paper.

AA

Academic Advising

Advisors help with course planning, degree requirements, registration, and choosing or changing a major.

Use it when: your course load feels unmanageable or you're unsure what to take next semester.

SA

Student Accessibility Services

Coordinates academic accommodations, such as testing adjustments, for students with disabilities or health conditions.

Use it when: a documented condition affects how you learn, take exams, or attend class.

How CampusCare works

1

Recognize

The bot identifies stress, burnout, overload, or exam anxiety in plain, supportive language.

2

Clarify

It asks a few simple questions to understand what the student is facing and what matters most.

3

Plan

It suggests small, realistic steps such as breaking tasks into manageable pieces or planning review time.

4

Connect

It helps students identify relevant USF resources and encourages professional help when appropriate.

Examples of student questions

  • “I feel overwhelmed with schoolwork. What should I do?”
  • “How can I reduce exam stress and improve my study routine?”
  • “What USF resource can help me with tutoring or academic advising?”
  • “I feel burned out. What healthy habits can help me regain balance?”

Designed for real student needs

  • Encourages healthy routines and realistic planning.
  • Helps students identify USF Counseling Center, Academic Success Center, advising, and accessibility support.
  • Addresses common challenges such as stress, anxiety, and overload.
  • Supports students with empathetic, action-oriented information.
In crisis or thinking about harming yourself? Call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) any time, or call 911 in an emergency.

CampusCare is a student project prototype, not an official University of South Florida service. It provides general information, not counseling or medical care. Students should confirm current services and contact details through USF's official website and contact a qualified professional when they need personal support.
